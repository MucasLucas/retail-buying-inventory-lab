discontinuation without margin, availability, and strategic-role data.

## Limitations

- Observed sales do not measure unconstrained demand.
- Gross sales do not measure net revenue or profit.
- Product availability and launch dates are unknown.
- Stock-code eligibility remains subject to review.
- One annual window does not establish repeatable seasonality.
- Large-order concentration does not establish customer type.
